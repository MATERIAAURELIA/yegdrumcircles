Questions

Holiday party drum circles: common questions

What are good staff Christmas party ideas in Edmonton?

Something everyone can do together beats something everyone watches. A facilitated drum circle gets the whole office playing one groove in minutes, mixes departments, and ends in laughter. It works as the opening act, the after-dinner moment, or the main event.

How long is a holiday party drum circle?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes. A shorter set fits between dinner and the rest of the evening; a longer one can include a few laughter and team challenges.

Is it an indoor activity?

Yes. We bring everything into your office, restaurant private room, hall or hotel ballroom. All you need is space for a circle of chairs.

Do people need any musical experience?

No. Drums are provided for every guest and everyone succeeds in the first few minutes. Bosses included.

How many people can join?

From small teams of 10 to company parties of 60 or more. Tell us your headcount and we'll plan the circle around it.

How far ahead should we book for December?

December dates fill first, so reach out as soon as you have a date. Call 780-257-6428 or request a quote and we'll check availability.

Do you travel outside Edmonton?

Yes, across the Edmonton area including St. Albert, Sherwood Park and Spruce Grove, and to Red Deer and Calgary for the right event.
